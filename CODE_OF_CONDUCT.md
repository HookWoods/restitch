# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Restitch to be a professional, respectful, and useful project for everyone who participates.

Project spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and any other place where the project is maintained.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, design, and tradeoffs.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about problems.
- Make space for maintainers to ask for smaller, clearer, or better tested changes.
- Respect boundaries when maintainers decline a proposal or defer it to a later release.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Repeated off-topic disruption after a maintainer asks for it to stop

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project community.

If you need to report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers through the repository owner profile or a private channel made available by the project.
